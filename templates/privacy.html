{{ company_name }} ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you participate in the Creator Gift Box Campaign.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to process your gift claim and deliver your package:

  • Personal Identification: Full name, email address, phone number.
  • Shipping Information: Country, city, address, postal code.
  • Campaign Details: YouTube channel name, claim code, clothing size.
✅ We DO NOT collect: Passwords, Gmail passwords, OTPs, banking PINs, credit card numbers, or any unnecessary financial information.

How We Use Your Information

  • Claim Processing: To verify your eligibility and process your claim.
  • Delivery: To ship your gift package to your provided address.
  • Communication: To send you claim confirmations, payment receipts, and delivery updates.
  • Campaign Administration: To maintain records of claims and payments.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third party.
  • We may share your information with trusted shipping providers solely for the purpose of delivering your package.
  • We never share your data with YouTube or Google.
  • We do not share your data with marketing or advertising partners.

Data Security

  • We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) to protect your data during transmission.
  • Your information is stored in a secure database with restricted access.
  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Retention

  • We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill your claim and comply with legal obligations.
  • Typically, we retain claim records for 3 years from the date of submission.
  • You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Your Rights

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Withdrawal: You may withdraw your claim at any time.

Cookies & Tracking

  • We use session cookies to maintain your claim session.
  • We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or analytics.
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

  • Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes to This Policy
